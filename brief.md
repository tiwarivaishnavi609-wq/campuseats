# CampusEats Brief

## What

CampusEats is a food ordering and management system designed for a college campus. It allows students to view food menus, place orders, make payments, and track their orders. The system helps cafeterias and food vendors manage orders efficiently and provide better service to students.

## Who Uses It

1. Students – to browse menus and order food.
2. Vendors/Cafeteria Staff – to receive and prepare orders.
3. Administrators – to manage users, vendors, and system operations.
